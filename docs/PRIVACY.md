# Privacy

Public listings contain only operator-approved display metadata: name, description, allowlisted tags, Minecraft/loader/e4steam compatibility, player count, capacity and required addon versions. They do not contain a Minecraft profile, Steam persona name, Steam ID, raw address or connection credential.

Owner references are opaque registry values. Reports use a keyed, non-reversible reporter fingerprint for duplicate suppression. Local hide/block lists remain local to the player. Expired operational rows are purged; moderation audit retention is bounded by backend configuration.
